# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technically curious readers visiting Sam's public notes, and Sam as the gardener maintaining an evolving body of linked ideas.

## Product Purpose

Make Sam's thinking easy and rewarding to wander through. The site should foreground notes and their relationships rather than presenting itself as a conventional chronological blog or a software documentation portal.

## Brand Personality

Thoughtful, candid, and lived-in. The experience should feel like a field notebook kept by an experienced engineer: precise where it matters, informal where it helps, and visibly personal.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic developer-blog polish, startup gradients, interchangeable card layouts, dashboard chrome, and cramped wiki density.

## Design Principles

- Make wandering feel intentional through clear trails between notes.
- Keep the writing dominant and comfortably readable.
- Let the garden feel tended, not finished or manufactured.
- Use technical precision without adopting corporate software aesthetics.
- Preserve Sam's direct and opinionated voice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and interaction expectations. Preserve visible keyboard focus, support reduced motion, and maintain comfortable reading sizes and line lengths across devices.
